# Tiệm Trà Nhỏ ☕

[Tiếng Việt](https://github.com/tiemtranho/tiemtranho/blob/main/README.md) · **English**

## About the game

Tiệm Trà Nhỏ is a tea shop management game. Stock ingredients, make each customer's drink to order, and grow your little shop day by day. A new version is in development; meanwhile, you can [play the current browser version](https://tiemtranho.com/game) on a phone or computer without installing anything.

## How to play

1. **Prepare your ingredients.** Open the inventory, buy tea, toppings, and cups, then prepare and stock them. Ingredients expire, so start with an amount you can sell.
2. **Read each order.** Check the tea, cup size (M or L), toppings, and flavors the customer requests.
3. **Make the drink.** Choose a cup, pour the right tea to the marked line, add toppings, and adjust sugar and ice when requested.
4. **Check and serve.** Seal the cup and serve before the customer runs out of patience. If you make a mistake, discard the drink and try again.
5. **Grow the shop.** Review customer ratings and your end-of-day results. Use your earnings to unlock more menu items and improve the shop.

The game also has an in-game **Hướng dẫn** (Guide) button with step-by-step instructions.
